Future Pro support

Support for planned Pro features must be documented before Pro becomes purchasable. No Pro account, checkout or entitlement flow exists here.

Mandatory limitation

TimeProofs AgentReady does not guarantee that an AI agent will never fail. It identifies structural risks that may cause AI agents to misuse APIs, tools or MCP servers.

AgentReady at a glance

Current support is self-service and repository-based; no response time, SLA, guaranteed fix or commercial support is promised. AgentReady is a product and candidate open standard for static pre-deployment analysis. It is not a runtime firewall, independent certification, official standards-body standard or guaranteed-safety system.

Availability
AgentReady Community is free and available. AgentReady Pro is planned and not purchasable.
