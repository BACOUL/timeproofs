FAQ

Is verification free?

Yes. Anyone can verify a proof file online or offline. Pricing applies to proof issuance capacity and operational trust.

Do you store any content?

No. TimeProofs is stateless. Only cryptographic hashes are used to produce proof files.

Why not cheaper?

Because this is infrastructure for evidence. The value is reliability, correctness, and long-term verifiability — not a consumer tool.

Does it prove authorship or intent?

No. It proves existence at a time and integrity over time. It does not prove identity, intent, legality, delivery, or success.
