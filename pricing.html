What you get when you pay

A proof is a small file you keep with your records. It supports later verification without relying on screenshots.

A proof file you keep

Each Freeze produces a portable proof file (.tproof.json) you store next to the document or in the case folder.

If the proof file is lost, it cannot be reconstructed in non-custodial mode.

Independent verification

Later, anyone can verify the proof against the content. If the content changed, it will not verify as the same proof.

FAQ

Is verification free?

Yes. Anyone can verify a proof online or offline. Pricing applies only to creating proofs beyond the free starter allowance.

Do you upload or store my documents?

No. Your original content stays on your device. TimeProofs produces a proof file you keep with your records.

Do I need a subscription?

No. You can use pay-as-you-go or packs. There is no monthly commitment.

What does Freeze prove?

Two facts: the exact content existed at a specific time, and it has not changed since. It does not prove authorship or intent.
