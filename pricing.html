What you get

A local scan, a human-readable Markdown report, and a machine-readable agentready.json contract that summarizes action types, risk levels, detected risks, confirmation requirements, and recommendations.

Boundaries: TimeProofs AgentReady does not guarantee that an AI agent will never fail. It identifies structural risks that may cause AI agents to misuse APIs, tools or MCP servers. Community works without a TimeProofs account, payment or backend. Pro remains in preparation.
