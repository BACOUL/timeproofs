Where the Freeze button appears

Freeze is designed to appear right before the moment you send. You click Freeze once, then you send normally.

Email

Before you send an email (with or without attachments), click Freeze to generate a proof of what you are about to send.

Best for: approvals, scope confirmations, sensitive messages, changes requests.

Documents

Before you send a quote, contract, or deliverable, click Freeze on the final file to create a proof you keep with your records.

Best for: quotes, SOW, contracts, “final” PDFs, exports.

Habit: if the document matters, Freeze it right before you send.
Later, verification is a deterministic check: either it matches, or it does not.

Keep the proof file

Store the proof file (.tproof.json) with your client folder or project records.

Important: TimeProofs is non-custodial. Keep the proof file with your records.
If the proof file is lost, it cannot be reconstructed in non-custodial mode.

What you get: a small proof file

A proof file is a portable receipt you can store and share. It is designed for later verification without relying on screenshots.

You can verify later by providing the content and the proof file. If the content was modified, it will not verify.

FAQ (installation + privacy)

Does TimeProofs upload my documents?

No. Your original content stays on your device. TimeProofs produces a proof file you keep with your records.

Does it replace my email or storage tools?

No. It adds a single action right before sending. You keep your existing workflow and tools.

Do I need an account or a subscription?

No account required. The economic model is designed to work without a subscription.

What does Verify do?

Verify checks an existing proof against the content to confirm it matches and has not changed since issuance.

Next: install, do one Freeze, keep the proof next to your client folder.
When a dispute happens, you verify the content against the proof. No screenshot trust.
