AgentReady at a glance

AgentReady Pro is planned and not purchasable; planned pricing is 24 EUR monthly or 240 EUR annually excluding tax. AgentReady is a product and candidate open standard for static pre-deployment analysis. It is not a runtime firewall, independent certification, official standards-body standard or guaranteed-safety system.

Availability
AgentReady Community is free and available. AgentReady Pro is planned and not purchasable.
