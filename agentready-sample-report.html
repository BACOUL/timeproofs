Report interpretation

A report is release evidence, not a safety certificate.

AgentReady reports combine score, status, severity counts, policy PASS/FAIL and rule-level findings so teams can decide whether to block, fix or accept a contract.

How to read this

The score describes readiness signals in the contract. The policy result describes the configured gate. A policy can fail because the score is too low, because a critical finding exists, or both.

For the refund fixture, the report highlights AR002_MISSING_CONFIRMATION_BOUNDARY, AR001_UNBOUNDED_WRITE_ACTION, AR006_MISSING_DRY_RUN_OR_PREVIEW, AR005_SENSITIVE_DATA_EXPOSURE and AR007_OVERBROAD_TOOL_SCOPE.

Use AR codes to assign remediation and detect repeat failures across releases.

3

Apply policy

Use min-score and fail-on to decide PASS or FAIL.

4

Document limits

Keep runtime, dynamic capabilities and human operating limits visible in release evidence.

TimeProofs AgentReady does not guarantee that an AI agent will never fail. It identifies structural risks that may cause AI agents to misuse APIs, tools or MCP servers.

Reports do not execute the target API, validate live identity controls or prove that a future agent will make correct decisions.
