Situations where verifiable proof is REQUIRED.

When data integrity and timing are critical to compliance, reliance on internal system logs is insufficient. Independent, verifiable evidence is REQUIRED.

AI Provenance Regulatory Audit Trade Secrets Software Supply Chain Out of Scope Compliance Boundary

1. AI Provenance & Liability

Context Automated decisions, Generative AI outputs, EU AI Act compliance.
Requirement Traceability. Operators must be able to reproduce or prove the state of the system and its output at the time of a specific decision.
Evidence Required Proof that an exact model output or dataset existed at time T and has not been altered since.
TimeProofs Role (Baseline) TimeProofs provides the minimal compliant evidence format. AI systems MUST retain a .tproof.json for critical outputs.

2. Regulatory Audit & Data Integrity

Context GDPR, Financial Logs, Healthcare Records (HIPAA/HDS).
Requirement Integrity and Non-Repudiation. Organizations MUST demonstrate that logs or records presented during an audit are identical to the originals.
Evidence Required A cryptographic anchor that validates the integrity of the log file independent of the storage medium.
TimeProofs Role (Baseline) Audit trails MUST be anchored. TimeProofs provides the portable artifact required for offline verification.

3. Intellectual Property & Trade Secrets

Context Data Act, NDA disclosures, Pre-patent research.
Requirement Proof of Possession. Claimants MUST demonstrate they possessed specific information prior to a disclosure date.
Evidence Required A "date certain" established by a third party, bound to the exact content hash.
TimeProofs Role (Baseline) TimeProofs creates the mandatory technical antecedent for legal defense of trade secrets.

4. Software Supply Chain

Context Secure Software Development Lifecycle (SSDLC), Release Management.
Requirement Tamper-Evidence. Consumers MUST be able to verify that the binary they run matches the binary that was built and approved.
Evidence Required Signed verification that the binary hash was finalized at the build time.
TimeProofs Role (Baseline) Release artifacts MUST be anchored. TimeProofs serves as the machine-readable integrity check in the supply chain.

Machine-to-Machine Integration

This standard is designed for machine-to-machine compliance.

  • Proof generation SHOULD be automated via API triggers.
  • Proof verification SHOULD be part of automated CI/CD or audit pipelines.
  • Systems MUST treat the absence of a valid proof file as a compliance failure.

Out of Scope

TimeProofs provides technical evidence of existence and integrity. It is important to define what is NOT covered by this standard:

  • Content Analysis: We do not verify the truthfulness, legality, or quality of the content. We verify that these specific bytes existed.
  • Identity Verification: We confirm that a proof was signed by a specific key at a specific time. We do not validate the real-world identity of the artifact creator unless integrated with a QTSP.
  • Data Storage: TimeProofs is stateless. We do not store, archive, or back up the original artifacts.

Compliance Boundary

Any system unable to produce independent, verifiable proof of existence for regulated artifacts cannot reasonably claim audit readiness nor regulatory compliance.
