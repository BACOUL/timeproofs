1) AI provenance (prompts, outputs)

What it solves. Prove origin and integrity for prompts and model outputs. Share a public verify URL without exposing content.

Why TimeProofs. Local hashing · Signed edge timestamp · Public verification.

// How it works
1) Client hashes prompt/output bytes (SHA-256).
2) POST /api/timestamp { hash, type:"ai", meta:{ model:"gpt-4o", run:"2025-11-07" } }
3) Share verify URL with users, auditors, or platforms.
# Snippet
HASH=$(printf "%s" "$OUTPUT" | sha256sum | awk '{print $1}')
curl -s https://api.timeproofs.io/api/timestamp \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d "{\"hash\":\"$HASH\",\"type\":\"ai\",\"meta\":{\"model\":\"my-model\"}}"

Compliance: EU AI Act (traceability). GDPR: hash-only, no content.

2) Legal & compliance audit trails

What it solves. Immutable proofs for filings, evidence chains, policies, NDAs, and disclosures.

Why TimeProofs. Time anchoring with public verification. Zero content retention.

// How it works
Hash each PDF/policy. Store the returned {hash|ts|sig}.
Publish verify URLs inside the case or policy dossier.

Compliance: GDPR (data minimization). eIDAS-like evidence: time-bound integrity.

3) DevOps & secure releases

What it solves. Build and artifact integrity across CI/CD. Release time proof.

Why TimeProofs. Edge timestamps <100 ms. Public verify page for each artifact hash.

# CI sample
HASH=$(sha256sum dist/app.min.js | awk '{print $1}')
curl -s https://api.timeproofs.io/api/timestamp \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d "{\"hash\":\"$HASH\",\"type\":\"release\",\"meta\":{\"source\":\"ci\"}}"

Compliance: NIS2/SBOM attestations with public verification.

Quick FAQ

Do you store my files? No. Only hashes. Original content never leaves your device.

Can anyone verify? Yes. Public verify endpoint or any ProofSpec client.

How fast? Typical edge latency under 100 ms.

Key rotation? Signatures support key IDs; verification remains stable.
