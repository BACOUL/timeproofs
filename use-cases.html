Top scenarios

AI provenance (prompts, outputs)

Hash prompts and outputs locally. Timestamp via API. Share verify URL to prove origin and integrity.

// flow
hash := sha256(prompt|output); POST /timestamp
verify := GET /verify?hash=...
share verify URL

Legal & compliance audit trails

Immutable event proofs for filings, evidence chains, and policy updates. Public verification without revealing content.

Helps with EU AI Act traceability and GDPR minimization.

DevOps & secure releases

Prove build integrity and release timing across CI/CD. Publish verification links in release notes.

# CI sample (bash)
HASH=$(sha256sum dist/app.min.js | awk '{print $1}')
curl -s https://api.timeproofs.io/api/timestamp \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d "{\"hash\":\"$HASH\",\"type\":\"release\",\"meta\":{\"source\":\"ci\"}}"

Creators & media certificates

Issue portable proof bundles for images, audio, and documents. The bundle contains the proof, not the content.

Data & research integrity

Anchor dataset snapshots and analysis notebooks. Allow peers to independently verify existence and immutability.

Supply chain transparency

Publish proofs for SBOMs, attestations, and vendor artifacts. Third parties can verify without accounts.
