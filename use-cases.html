Use Freeze when “this version” matters.

TimeProofs is for the moment right before you send a quote, contract, deliverable, or sensitive email. You keep a small proof file with your records. Verification stays possible later — independently.

No upload. No storage. Verifiable later.

Shortcut: Install once, then click Freeze right before sending.
Installation instructions are now on the dedicated page: Install →

When to click Freeze

Use it at the exact moment where “this version” matters:

Before sending a quote or pricing

Freeze the quote you are about to send. If the price is challenged later, you can verify the exact document that existed when you clicked.

Before sending a contract or SOW

Freeze the version you share for signature or approval. If terms are disputed later, you can show what existed at time T.

Before approving changes by email

Freeze the email content or attached file before you confirm scope, timeline, or deliverables.

Before delivering “final” files

Freeze the final PDF or deliverable you send. If the version is questioned later, you can verify what you delivered.

If you currently rely on file dates or screenshots, Freeze is the safer step.

Common disputes it prevents

These disputes are costly because they consume time, trust, and money.

“This is not the version you sent.”

Freeze creates a verifiable proof tied to the exact content that existed when you clicked.

“The price was different.”

Freeze the quote before sending. Later, verification shows the quote is unchanged since that timestamp.

“We never agreed on that scope.”

Freeze the SOW or the email where scope is confirmed. The proof supports what existed at time T.

“You changed the document after.”

Verification detects any modification. If content differs, it will not verify as the same proof.

What Freeze proves: (1) the exact content existed at a specific time, and (2) it has not changed since.
It does not prove who authored it or the intent behind it.

What you get after clicking Freeze

A small proof file you keep with your records. This is what makes verification possible later.

How it works

Three steps: Freeze → Keep the proof → Verify later.

1) Freeze

Select the file (or content) you are about to send. Your original stays on your device.

2) Keep the proof file

TimeProofs generates a small proof file (.tproof.json) for your records.

3) Verify later

Anyone can verify integrity later using the proof file and the content. If content changed, it won’t verify.

Verification does not rely on screenshots or “trust me”.
It is a check based on the proof file and the content you present.

Principles

Private by design

Your original content is not uploaded. You keep your file. The proof is small and shareable.

Non-custodial

TimeProofs is not storage. You keep your proof file. This avoids long-term dependency.

Independent verification

Verification is a check anyone can run. It does not rely on a screenshot.
