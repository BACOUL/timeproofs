Legal & Compliance

Evidence of prior existence and integrity for contracts, policies, annexes, and regulatory disclosures.

  • Sign a timestamp for each version of ToS/Privacy. Link the proof on-page.
  • Hash annexes and compliance matrices. Publish verification URLs in the dossier.
  • Retain public verification routes for third parties.

DevOps & Releases

Prove build integrity and release timing across CI/CD.

# CI sample (bash)
HASH=$(sha256sum dist/app.min.js | awk '{print $1}')
curl -s https://api.timeproofs.io/api/timestamp \
  -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  -d "{\"hash\":\"$HASH\",\"type\":\"release\",\"meta\":{\"artifact\":\"app.min.js\"}}"

Add the returned hash|ts|sig to release notes and expose a “Verify” link.

Creators & Media

Certify authorship moments for images, videos, songs, and posts without uploading originals.

  • Hash the file locally in your app, never send the content.
  • Store the verification URL alongside the asset metadata.
  • Display the “Verified by TimeProofs” badge on pages and repos.

Supply Chain

Anchor digital events along the chain: purchase orders, scans, certificates, and handoffs.

  • Use event proofs for each checkpoint. Keep payloads local. Share only verification links.
  • Allow partners to verify without contacting your API if you expose static proof bundles later.
