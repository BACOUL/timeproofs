Scope

This page applies to: (1) the website timeproofs.io, (2) the reference API at api.timeproofs.io, and (3) the ProofSpec protocol materials and portable proof bundles (e.g., .tproof.json).

What TimeProofs proves

  • A specific hash existed at a specific timestamp (issued by the service).
  • The proof can be verified later against the same hash without revealing the original content.

What TimeProofs does not prove

  • Identity of an author or owner of a file/dataset.
  • Copyright ownership, licensing rights, or transfer of rights.
  • Delivery, reading, acceptance, intent, or consent.
  • That the original content was lawful, accurate, or authentic.
