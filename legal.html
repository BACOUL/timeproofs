Legal / limitations

TimeProofs is a traceability artifact, not a legal verdict.

TimeProofs helps prove technical integrity and Seal consistency for AI Action Files. It does not prove that the AI was correct, that an action was lawful, or that a third party must accept the record.

What TimeProofs can technically show

Integrity

Payload hash consistency

The verifier can compare a recomputed Action File payload hash with the payload hash referenced in the Seal.

Issuer

Seal signature status

The verifier can check whether a Seal signature matches the referenced public key and signed Seal payload.

Trace

Structured record coherence

An Action File can make an AI action easier to inspect, share, archive, and reconstruct later.

What TimeProofs does not prove

  • TimeProofs does not prove that the AI output was correct, complete, accurate, safe, or optimal.
  • TimeProofs does not guarantee the legal validity of the underlying business action.
  • TimeProofs does not prove that a person had authority, consent, intent, or capacity to perform an action.
  • TimeProofs does not prove that the original evidence was truthful, complete, lawful, or properly collected.
  • TimeProofs does not guarantee that a court, regulator, insurer, bank, auditor, customer, supplier, or partner will accept the record.
  • TimeProofs does not provide notarization, legal certification, regulated audit, or court-ready assurance.

Proof levels are evidence labels, not legal levels

Proof levels describe the technical strength of the traceability record. They do not decide legal value, compliance status, enforceability, admissibility, liability, or commercial acceptance.

Proof levelTechnical meaningLegal limit
declaredAn action was declared in an Action File.Does not prove execution or legal validity.

Compliance and audit boundaries

  • TimeProofs can support auditability and traceability, but it is not a full AI Act compliance product.
  • TimeProofs can support data minimization, but it is not a full GDPR compliance guarantee.
  • TimeProofs does not replace DPIA, risk assessment, vendor due diligence, internal policy, legal review, or security audit.
  • Customer teams remain responsible for lawful basis, retention, access control, data quality, and governance of their own Action Files.

Customer responsibilities

Action content

The customer is responsible for the action, the evidence, the workflow configuration, and the information placed in the Action File.

Storage and sharing

The customer is responsible for storing, retaining, deleting, and sharing Action Files according to its own policies and obligations.

Sensitive data

The customer should avoid placing personal data, secrets, raw prompts, raw outputs, credentials, or confidential content in fields where hashes or redacted summaries are enough.

Use in disputes

The customer should obtain professional advice before relying on an Action File in a legal, regulatory, contractual, employment, insurance, banking, or procurement dispute.

Acceptable use

  • Do not use TimeProofs to misrepresent an AI action, hide relevant context, or claim a legal status that the product does not provide.
  • Do not use TimeProofs to seal unlawful, abusive, infringing, discriminatory, deceptive, or harmful workflows.
  • Do not attempt to overload, bypass, scrape, or attack the service or its verification endpoints.
  • Do not include passwords, private keys, tokens, API keys, or other secrets in optional metadata.

Availability and changes

V1 pilot features, public pages, examples, schemas, SDK helpers, and API behavior may change. Unless a written agreement states otherwise, no uptime SLA, regulated certification, or acceptance guarantee is provided.
