Normative language

  • Service: the websites, official APIs, SDK/CLI, offline/local tools, and any hosted UI operated under timeproofs.io and its subdomains.
  • Hash: irreversible digest (typically SHA-256) of input content.
  • Proof: record attesting that a hash existed at a specific time and was signed by TimeProofs.
  • Bundle (.tproof.json): JSON document that packages proof fields plus protocol metadata.
  • Meta: optional small JSON you provide. It MUST NOT contain personal data or sensitive information.
