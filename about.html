About TimeProofs

TimeProofs answers a simple question: when did this data exist, and has it changed? You hash locally, receive a signed .tproof.json proof, and anyone can verify it — without seeing your data.

Mission

What TimeProofs proves

Two facts only: when a specific piece of content existed (via its hash), and that it has not changed since.

It does not prove origin, intent, authorship, delivery, or acceptance.

Existence Integrity

Why it matters

In the AI era, content and data change fast. Trust requires independent verification — not platform trust, not editable timestamps.

What TimeProofs does — and does not do

It does

  • Timestamp a hash and return a signed .tproof.json proof bundle.
  • Enable public verification via POST /api/verify (bundle required).
  • Enable offline verification with a ProofSpec-compliant verifier.

Signed proof Verifiable

It does not

  • Store your files, messages, prompts, or datasets.
  • Prove authorship, originality, or whether something was delivered/read.
  • Require a blockchain or expose your underlying content.

No storage No blockchain
