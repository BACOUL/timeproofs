About TimeProofs

TimeProofs is built as a neutral, privacy-first proof layer: hash locally, receive a signed .tproof.json bundle, and verify it online or offline — without exposing your data.

Mission

What TimeProofs proves

Two facts only: when a specific piece of content existed (via its hash), and that it has not changed since.

It does not prove origin, intent, authorship, delivery, or acceptance.

Existence Integrity

Why now

In the AI era, content and data change fast. Trust requires independent verification — not platform trust, not screenshots, not editable timestamps.

What TimeProofs does — and does not do

It does

  • Timestamp a hash and return a signed .tproof.json bundle.
  • Enable public verification via /api/verify.
  • Enable offline verification using a ProofSpec-compliant verifier.

Signed bundle Verifiable

It does not

  • Store your files, messages, prompts, or datasets.
  • Prove authorship, originality, or whether something was delivered/read.
  • Require a blockchain or expose your underlying content.

No storage No blockchain
