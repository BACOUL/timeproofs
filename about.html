About TimeProofs

Mission, values, governance, and transparency behind TimeProofs — the open, privacy-first standard for digital proof of existence, compatible with a stateless API v0.2 and portable .tproof.json proof files.

Mission & vision

Mission. Provide a simple, verifiable way to prove when specific data existed and that it has not changed, without revealing the original content.

Vision. TimeProofs is built as a reference implementation of an open protocol (ProofSpec) intended to become a neutral standard for digital proof of existence.

Hash locally · Timestamp via API · Verify publicly · No blockchain · No tokens.

New in v0.2

v0.2 keeps the same core promise (hash-only proofs) while improving portability and integration:

  • Stateless API. Proofs can be issued without requiring accounts or dashboards as a dependency.
  • Portable .tproof.json bundles. Machine-readable proofs you can store alongside artifacts and logs.
  • Offline inspection. Proof bundles remain inspectable without needing the original content.

Core values

Privacy-first

Only hashes are processed. The protocol does not require your original files, prompts, datasets, or documents.

Open and neutral

The protocol is designed to be implementable by others, without lock-in or proprietary formats.

Verifiability over claims

Trust should come from artifacts that can be verified independently, not from marketing.

Developer-first simplicity

Integrations should be lightweight: a curl call, a small SDK function, or a CI step.

What it does

  1. Hash locally. You hash the exact bytes you want to prove (often SHA-256) on your side.
  2. Get a signed timestamp. You send the hash to an issuer which returns a signed proof with a UTC timestamp.
  3. Verify later. Anyone can validate the proof without seeing the original data.

TimeProofs is an implementation; ProofSpec is the protocol others can also implement.

Governance & roadmap

TimeProofs is maintained as a reference implementation of ProofSpec, with an open-standard roadmap aimed at broader community governance over time.

Versions evolve incrementally (v0.1 → v0.2 → …) without breaking already-issued proofs.

Trust & transparency

TimeProofs is designed to minimize hidden data flows: hash-only proofs, minimal logs, clear security posture, and open verification paths.

Security posture, disclosure process, and operational details are documented on the Security page.

Who it is for

AI & ML teams. Seal prompts, outputs, datasets, and audit trails without leaking content.

Developers & DevOps. Seal build artifacts, releases, configs, and deployment histories.

Legal & compliance. Attach verifiable timestamps to documents and evidence trails.

Researchers & creators. Publish work with proof bundles that certify existence at a point in time.
