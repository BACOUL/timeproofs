Section 2Why it matters now

AI systems can rewrite, generate, and remix faster than humans can react. In this infinite remix era, trust begins with an anchor in time. Proof of existence is becoming the new HTTPS — a baseline for authenticity, authorship, and audit.

Section 3What TimeProofs does

How it works

1) Hash locally. Compute SHA-256 on any artifact.

2) Timestamp via API. Send the hash, get back a timestamp + signature.

3) Verify publicly. Confirm existence at /api/verify?hash=….

Why it’s different

• No uploads — privacy by design
• No blockchain — instant & predictable
• Edge-native — worldwide latency
• Public verify — open & transparent

Section 4How it compares

Other systems rely on blockchains (slow, costly), e-sign platforms (heavy, proprietary), or content metadata (fragile, inconsistent). TimeProofs is a neutral layer that fits anywhere — for AI outputs, datasets, contracts, or research.

Section 5The bigger picture

By 2030, AI-generated data will exceed human content. The web will need a neutral timestamp backbone — a proof of when something first existed. That’s what TimeProofs is building.
