How it works

From AI action to verifiable Action File.

TimeProofs turns one observable AI action into a structured record that can be hashed, sealed, saved, and verified later without sending sensitive action content by default.

AI action→Action File→payload_hash→Seal→Verify
Important boundary. Verification checks integrity and Seal authenticity. It does not prove AI correctness, legal validity, regulatory compliance, business approval, or third-party acceptance.

The five-step flow

1
AI action

An AI assistant, automation, or agent performs or prepares a specific observable action.

2
Action File

The action is described in a structured .action.json file with actor, action, target system, proof level, evidence references, and limits.

3
payload_hash

The stable Action File core is canonicalized and hashed locally. Mutable fields and Seal fields stay outside the hashable core.

4
Seal

TimeProofs signs the payload hash and minimal non-sensitive metadata. Raw prompt, output, documents, files, tokens, and secrets should not be sent by default.

5
Verify

A verifier recomputes the payload hash and checks the Seal signature with the public key to detect modification or unsupported signatures.

What the company keeps

Customer-owned record

The company keeps the generated Action File and any private evidence in its own systems. It can attach the Action File to a CRM event, support ticket, procurement file, report, or internal review.

Privacy-first evidence model

TimeProofs is designed so sensitive content can stay outside the TimeProofs service. The Seal can be based on a canonical fingerprint of the action record.

What verification does not say

  • It does not say the AI answer was true or optimal.
  • It does not say a human approved the business decision unless the Action File records that separately.
  • It does not say a regulator, court, customer, insurer, or auditor will accept the record.
  • It does not replace logs, SIEM, monitoring, governance tools, legal review, or compliance controls.
