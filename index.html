Freeze before sending.

Create a verifiable proof of what you sent — privately, in one click. When a quote, contract, or email is disputed later, you can show the exact version that existed at that time.

Simple flow: Prepare → Freeze → Send → Verify later
TimeProofs does not replace your email or file tools. It adds a single action right before you send.

When to click Freeze

Use it at the exact moment where “this version” matters:

Before sending a quote or pricing

Freeze the quote you are about to send. If the price is challenged later, you can show the exact document that existed when you sent it.

Before sending a contract or SOW

Freeze the version you share for signature or approval. If terms change later, you can prove what the other party received at time T.

Before approving changes by email

Freeze the email content or attached file before you confirm “OK” on scope, timeline, or deliverables.

Before delivering “final” files

Freeze the final PDF, export, or deliverable you send to a client. If a version is questioned later, you can prove what was delivered.

If you currently rely on file dates or screenshots, Freeze is the safer step.

Common disputes it prevents

These disputes are not rare. They are expensive because they cost time, trust, and money.

“This is not the version you sent.”

Freeze creates a verifiable proof tied to the exact content that existed when you clicked.

“The price was different.”

Freeze the quote before sending. Later, verification shows the quote is unchanged since that timestamp.

“We never agreed on that scope.”

Freeze the SOW or the email where scope is confirmed. The proof supports what existed at time T.

“You changed the document after.”

Verification detects any modification. If content differs, it will not verify as the same proof.

What Freeze proves: (1) the exact content existed at a specific time, and (2) it has not changed since.
It does not prove who authored it or the intent behind it.

How it works

A single action before you send. Verification remains possible later — even for third parties.

1

Click Freeze

Select the file (or content) you are about to send. Your original stays on your device.

2

Get a proof file

TimeProofs generates a small proof file (.tproof.json) you can store with your records.

3

Verify later

Anyone can verify integrity later using the proof file and the content. No screenshot trust required.

For installation and workflow examples, see Use Cases →

Designed for real-world proof

Private by design

Your original content is not uploaded. You keep your file. The proof is small and shareable.

Verifiable independently

Verification is a check anyone can run. It does not rely on “trust me” or a screenshot.

Non-custodial

TimeProofs is not a storage service. You keep your proof file. This avoids long-term dependency.

Important: keep the proof file (.tproof.json) with your records.
If the proof file is lost, it cannot be reconstructed in non-custodial mode.
