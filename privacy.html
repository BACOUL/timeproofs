Privacy

TimeProofs is privacy-first by design: we process hashes, not content. Files and text never upload, and verification works without tracking.

Privacy at a glance

  • No content upload. Originals never leave your device.
  • Hash-only. We process SHA-256 hashes and proof metadata.
  • No accounts. No identity required to create or verify proofs.
  • No tracking cookies. We do not use marketing cookies.
  • Public verification. Anyone can verify a proof without logging in.

Important: a hash can sometimes be linkable to a person if the original content is highly unique and disclosed elsewhere. Avoid placing personal data in public metadata.

Principles

  • Minimization. We process only what is required to provide proof and prevent abuse.
  • No content. Files, text, and datasets are not uploaded or stored by TimeProofs.
  • Transparency. Public verification endpoints and an open specification.

What we process

FieldExamplePurpose
hash[a-f0-9]{64}Deterministic identifier for proof of existence and integrity checks
typeeventProof category (e.g., event)
meta{"source":"docs"}Optional context. Do not include personal data.
timestamp2025-01-01T00:00:00.000ZWhen the proof was issued
signature dataHMAC-SHA256Verification and auditability (issuer / alg / kid)

v0.2 returns self-contained .tproof.json bundles. Your bundle can be stored by you and verified later (offline or online).

What we do not process

  • No original files, text bodies, prompts, outputs, datasets, or attachments.
  • No user accounts or identity profiles.
  • No behavioral tracking, fingerprinting, or cross-site identifiers.
  • No sale of personal data.

Analytics

We use Plausible Analytics to understand aggregated website traffic. We do not use marketing cookies.

  • Purpose: measure page views and referrals to improve documentation and UX.
  • Scope: aggregated statistics, not personal profiles.

If you block analytics at the browser or network level, the site still works.

Cookies & Service Worker

  • Cookies: TimeProofs does not use cookies for authentication.
  • Local storage: may be used for minimal UI state (e.g., menu), not tracking.
  • Service Worker: used to cache static assets for performance/offline UX. It does not read your local files.

Logs & retention

  • Operational logs: minimal metrics for reliability and abuse prevention (rotated periodically).
  • Proof verification: public verification relies on hash-based records and signatures.

Recommendation: avoid personal data in meta. If you need to bind a proof to an identity, do it outside TimeProofs (e.g., signed documents) and timestamp only the hash.

Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights related to personal data (access, deletion, objection). TimeProofs is designed to avoid collecting personal data.

If you believe a record relates to you, contact us with the relevant hash and context. We respond consistent with applicable law.
