Privacy policy

How TimeProofs AgentReady handles files and personal data.

Local file analysis

The Browser V1 scanner is designed to analyze OpenAPI and MCP files locally in the browser. The static scanner pages do not need to upload the selected files to a TimeProofs backend.

No account by default

The current Browser V1 surface does not include accounts, user dashboards, payments, or a database.

Hosting and technical logs

When the site is visited through a hosting provider, technical access logs may be processed by the hosting provider for security, performance, and abuse prevention. Production hosting details should be confirmed before public launch.

Cookies

The current static product surface does not require advertising cookies or analytics cookies to perform a scan. If analytics or third-party services are added later, this policy must be updated before public launch.

Contact and rights

Contact details for privacy requests must be completed before a public production launch.
