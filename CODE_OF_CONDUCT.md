# Code of Conduct — TimeProofs

## Our Pledge
We, as members, contributors, and maintainers of TimeProofs, pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We commit to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

---

## Our Standards

### Examples of positive behavior
- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward others
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing opinions
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully
- Focusing on what is best for the project and the broader community

### Unacceptable behavior
- Harassment, discrimination, or derogatory comments
- Publishing private information without permission (“doxxing”)
- Personal attacks, insults, trolling, or deliberate intimidation
- Sustained disruption of discussions or collaboration
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Advocating for, encouraging, or glorifying violence or hate against any person or group

---

## Enforcement Responsibilities
Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct.  
They may remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate these standards.

---

## Scope
This Code applies to all project spaces, including:
- GitHub issues
- Pull Requests
- Discussions
- Community channels
- Public interactions where someone represents the project

---

## Enforcement

### Reporting
If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it to:
- **General contact:** contact@timeproofs.io
- **Security-related issues:** security@timeproofs.io

Reports will be handled as confidentially as possible.

### Enforcement Guidelines
Maintainers will follow these community impact guidelines:

1. **Correction** — A private, clear warning explaining the violation and expectations.
2. **Warning** — A formal warning with consequences for continued behavior.
3. **Temporary Ban** — A temporary restriction from contributing or participating.
4. **Permanent Ban** — A permanent removal from the community spaces.

Maintainers may skip steps depending on severity or repeated violations.

### Good-faith reports
Reports made in good faith are always welcome. Deliberately false or malicious reports may result in enforcement action.
