Pilot format

Step 1

Pick one workflow

Choose one repeated AI action where traceability is useful: drafted response, CRM update, supplier check, document routing, or operational automation.

Step 2

Generate Action Files

Produce a small set of customer-owned .action.json files for real or simulated actions, with sensitive content replaced by hashes or summaries where needed.

Step 3

Verify the Seal

Check whether the payload hash and TimeProofs Seal remain consistent. The pilot measures traceability value, not AI correctness.

What to send in the first message

  • Company name and website.
  • Role and contact email.
  • The AI workflow you want to trace.
  • Tools involved: CRM, helpdesk, agent, workflow automation, document system, or internal app.
  • Approximate number of AI actions per week or month.
  • What sensitive content should never leave your environment.

No heavy commitment for the first discussion

No account or dashboard required

The V1 pilot discussion does not require building accounts, user roles, CRM pipelines, or a full SaaS dashboard.

No payment on this page

This page does not process payments. Pricing and pilot offers are discussed separately after the workflow is qualified.

No sensitive upload by default

The target model is hash-only sealing by default. Raw prompts, outputs, documents, credentials, tokens, private keys, or personal data should not be sent by default.

No compliance promise

A pilot can support traceability evaluation, but it does not guarantee legal proof, AI correctness, court readiness, or full AI Act/GDPR compliance.

Good pilot candidates

  • Support teams: AI drafts customer responses or closes ticket actions.
  • Sales teams: AI creates CRM notes, outreach drafts, or account updates.
  • Procurement teams: AI helps compare suppliers, documents, or risk checks.
  • Operations teams: AI triggers recurring internal actions that need traceability.
  • Agencies and integrators: AI workflows are delivered to clients and need explainable proof files.
