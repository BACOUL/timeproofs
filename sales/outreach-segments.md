# TimeProofs Sales — Multisegment Outreach Plan

Phase: G — market validation and pilot prospecting  
Campaign size: 50 contacts minimum  
Primary goal: validate willingness to pay before building the full SaaS

## Campaign principle

Do not validate TimeProofs only with AI agencies.

AI agencies and no-code integrators are a fast entry channel, but the final market is broader: companies, technical teams, risk/legal/ops stakeholders, developers and platforms that use AI systems capable of taking business actions.

## Validation split

| Segment | Contact count | Main question |
|---|---:|---|
| AI agencies / no-code integrators | 20 | Can they resell Action File traceability into client AI workflows? |
| Companies already using AI workflows | 15 | Do they feel operational risk when AI creates or changes business records? |
| Developers / AI builders | 10 | Is the Action File + Seal + Verify integration clear and useful? |
| Risk / legal / compliance / operations | 5 | Does portable AI action traceability solve a real governance problem? |
| **Total** | **50** | Is there enough willingness to pay to continue building? |

## Segment 1 — AI agencies / no-code integrators

### Target profile

- AI automation agencies
- no-code automation studios
- Make consultants
- n8n consultants
- Zapier consultants
- chatbot integrators
- CRM automation consultants
- AI workflow builders for SMEs and mid-market companies

### Why this segment matters

One agency can deploy TimeProofs across several client workflows. This makes agencies a fast validation channel for pilots, templates and repeatable delivery.

### Best-fit workflows

- AI chatbot creates a CRM lead
- AI assistant creates a support ticket
- AI sends or drafts a client email
- AI generates a commercial or internal document
- AI updates a CRM record
- AI classifies a request and triggers an automation
- AI routes an incident or escalation

### Outreach angle

Add a black box to the AI workflows you deliver.

Your clients are starting to ask what the AI did, when it did it and what record was created. TimeProofs adds a portable Action File after each important AI action, without uploading sensitive client content by default.

### Qualification questions

1. Do your client AI workflows create, update, send or classify business records?
2. When an AI workflow goes wrong, how does the client reconstruct what happened?
3. Do your clients ask for proof, logs, auditability or traceability?
4. Could traceability be sold as an add-on to your AI workflow delivery?
5. Which workflow would be easiest to pilot first?

### Strong buying signals

- asks for Agency Kit, pricing or resale model
- names one client workflow immediately
- asks for Make, n8n or Zapier implementation pattern
- wants a client-facing one-pager
- wants a pilot for an existing client

### Objections to track

- “Our clients do not ask for this yet.”
- “Logs are enough.”
- “This needs to be built into Make/n8n/Zapier directly.”
- “We need a dashboard first.”
- “We cannot sell this as a separate line item.”
- “Privacy or legal claims need to be very clear.”

## Segment 2 — Companies already using AI workflows

### Target profile

Companies that already use AI in operational workflows, especially:

- support teams
- sales ops
- customer success
- back-office teams
- operations teams
- document-heavy SMEs
- teams using AI inside CRM, helpdesk, ERP, email or workflow automation

### Why this segment matters

This is the direct buyer market. These organizations feel the operational risk when AI actions affect customers, records, emails, documents or internal decisions.

### Best-fit workflows

- support ticket creation or classification
- CRM lead creation
- CRM field update
- automated customer response
- quote or proposal draft generation
- contract, report or document generation
- internal request routing
- payment, billing or admin case escalation

### Outreach angle

When AI takes an action in your business, can you prove what happened later?

TimeProofs creates a clear, signed and verifiable Action File for important AI actions. Your company keeps the file; TimeProofs seals only the fingerprint by default.

### Qualification questions

1. Where is AI already used in your operations?
2. Does AI create or update business records?
3. Who checks what happened when an AI action is wrong or disputed?
4. Are current logs readable by operations, management, legal or clients?
5. Would a portable Action File help with incident reconstruction or client trust?

### Strong buying signals

- mentions incident, dispute, client request, audit or internal concern
- asks where Action Files are stored
- asks about privacy and what TimeProofs receives
- identifies a concrete workflow owner
- asks for a pilot price

### Objections to track

- “We only use AI for brainstorming.”
- “Our AI does not take action.”
- “The IT team already has logs.”
- “This is a legal/compliance topic; I am not the owner.”
- “We need to see a demo with our own workflow.”

## Segment 3 — Developers / AI builders

### Target profile

- AI application developers
- solo AI builders
- product engineers
- internal automation developers
- API and workflow engineers
- developers building agentic workflows
- developers using OpenAI tools, LangChain, n8n, Make, CrewAI or custom agents

### Why this segment matters

Developers validate whether the Action File + canonical payload hash + Seal + Verify model is clear, implementable and technically credible.

### Best-fit workflows

- AI tool call creates a record
- AI agent triggers an external API action
- AI generates a document and stores it
- AI classifies an inbound request and opens a ticket
- AI routes a task to another system
- AI writes into CRM, helpdesk or database

### Outreach angle

Add a small traceability layer after your AI tool calls.

TimeProofs lets your app generate a business-readable Action File, hash the canonical payload locally, seal the hash and verify it later.

### Qualification questions

1. Are you building AI workflows that call tools or external APIs?
2. Do you need a portable record of what the AI action did?
3. Would you prefer a small API/SDK or a dashboard-first product?
4. Is canonical payload hashing understandable and acceptable?
5. What would make the integration too heavy?

### Strong buying signals

- asks for SDK, code sample or OpenAPI details
- asks about canonical JSON, JCS/RFC 8785 or signature verification
- names an agent/tool-call workflow
- asks about offline verification or key rotation
- wants to test a proof file

### Objections to track

- “This should be open-source only.”
- “I can build this myself.”
- “I need a library, not a web app.”
- “I need offline verification first.”
- “I need API keys and usage limits before using it.”

## Segment 4 — Risk / legal / compliance / operations

### Target profile

- risk managers
- legal operations
- compliance officers
- internal audit
- operations managers
- AI governance stakeholders
- security and trust stakeholders

### Why this segment matters

These stakeholders validate whether TimeProofs solves a governance problem beyond developer logs.

### Best-fit concerns

- AI incident reconstruction
- evidence retention
- customer dispute review
- internal audit preparation
- AI governance controls
- separation between developer logs and business evidence
- traceability of automated actions

### Outreach angle

Technical logs help developers. TimeProofs creates a portable business artifact that helps non-technical teams understand what an AI action claimed to do, when it happened and how it can be verified.

### Qualification questions

1. Do you have AI workflows that create operational risk?
2. Who investigates an AI action if a customer, manager or auditor asks what happened?
3. Are developer logs enough for non-technical review?
4. Would a signed Action File help with internal governance?
5. What limitation language would be required before a pilot?

### Strong buying signals

- asks about proof levels
- asks about what is verified and what is not
- asks about storage, retention or audit trail
- asks about logs vs portable Action Files
- wants legal limitation language before testing

### Objections to track

- “This is not legal proof.”
- “This needs certification.”
- “This needs full compliance review.”
- “We need self-hosting.”
- “We need internal policy first.”

## Contact scoring

Score each contact from 0 to 5.

| Score | Meaning | Action |
|---:|---|---|
| 0 | No relevance | Do not follow up |
| 1 | Weak curiosity | Send short explanation only |
| 2 | Real question or mild relevance | Follow up once |
| 3 | Serious reply with real workflow | Ask for call |
| 4 | Qualified call or pilot discussion | Push pilot offer |
| 5 | Paid pilot / strong equivalent proof | Prioritize delivery |

For every contact, record:

- contact_id
- segment
- company / person
- role
- source
- contact date
- channel
- current AI workflow
- workflow action type
- pain category
- reply status
- serious reply yes/no
- qualified call yes/no
- pilot status
- price discussed
- objection category
- exact objection wording
- next action
- owner
- notes

## Campaign cadence

### Week 1

- prepare 50-contact target list
- send first 20 messages
- prioritize AI agencies and companies already using AI workflows
- schedule first calls

### Week 2

- complete the 50-contact outreach
- follow up on non-replies
- run qualified calls
- identify top 3 workflows

### Week 3

- push pilot offer to highest-scoring prospects
- record objections by segment
- adjust pitch only if repeated confusion appears
- avoid building new SaaS features

### Week 4

- close paid pilots or strong equivalent proof
- complete GO_NO_GO.md decision template
- decide: GO / PIVOT / STOP

## Outreach rule

Do not pitch TimeProofs as a broad compliance product.

Pitch one concrete idea:

When AI performs an important business action, TimeProofs creates a portable, signed and verifiable Action File. The company keeps the file. TimeProofs seals the fingerprint.
