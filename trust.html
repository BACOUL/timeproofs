Mandatory limitation

TimeProofs AgentReady does not guarantee that an AI agent will never fail. It identifies structural risks that may cause AI agents to misuse APIs, tools or MCP servers.

AgentReady at a glance

The Trust Center links the actual security model, privacy boundaries, disclosure channel, legal notice, terms, limitations and support boundaries. AgentReady is a product and candidate open standard for static pre-deployment analysis. It is not a runtime firewall, independent certification, official standards-body standard or guaranteed-safety system.

Availability
AgentReady Community is free and available. AgentReady Pro is planned and not purchasable.
